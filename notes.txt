receive live order-book data
        ↓
maintain local order books
        ↓
calculate executable prices/liquidity
        ↓
detect arbitrage / mispricing
        ↓
estimate profit after fees + slippage
        ↓
rank opportunities

Yes order book
No order book

best executable Yes price
best executable No price
        ↓
compare against $1 payout relationship
